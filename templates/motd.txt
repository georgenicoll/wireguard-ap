wireguard-ap scripts (in ~, run without sudo - they escalate themselves where needed):

  setup_ap.sh dual|uplink [5|2.4]        (Re)configure the AP. Safe to re-run, including to switch modes.
  uplink_wifi.sh "SSID" ["password"]     Join an upstream Wi-Fi network on wlan0 (uplink mode only).
  view_currently_associated_clients.sh   Show devices, routes, AP status and connected clients.
  setup_forwarding_and_nat.sh            (Re)apply IP forwarding and NAT. Usually only needed once.

Current settings: ~/wireguard-ap.env
Full docs: https://github.com/georgenicoll/wireguard-ap
